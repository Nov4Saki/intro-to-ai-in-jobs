Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Data Protection Clause (Vendor: Enterprise Cloud Suite 2026)

1. Service Availability & Uptime Guarantee:
The Provider guarantees a monthly service availability of ninety-nine point ninety-five percent (99.95%), excluding scheduled maintenance windows pre-approved by the Customer with a minimum forty-eight (48) hours prior written notice.

2. Incident Resolution Times (RTO & RPO):
In the event of an unplanned service disruption classified as Critical (Severity 1), the Provider commits to a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of not more than four (4) consecutive hours, and a Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of not more than fifteen (15) minutes of data transaction loss.

3. Data Sovereignty & Zero-Retention Artificial Intelligence Clause:
All Customer data, queries, logs, and artifacts processed via automated or generative AI modules shall remain strictly encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3) within national data borders. The Provider expressly warrants that Customer inputs shall not be retained, cached beyond session termination, or utilized for foundational model retraining.

4. Service Credits & Liquidated Damages:
Failure to achieve the stipulated monthly uptime SLA shall entitle the Customer to a pro-rata service credit calculated at five percent (5%) of the monthly recurring fee for each cumulative hour of unexcused outage, capped at fifty percent (50%) of the applicable billing cycle.
