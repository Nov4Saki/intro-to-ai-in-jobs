# AI at Work Classroom Activities

Interactive workshop activities accompanying the Arabic "Introduction to AI in Daily Work" training series for university students and professionals.

## Language

### Workshop structure

**Session**:
A scheduled workshop unit in the five-part training series, grouping the activities used during that class.
_Avoid_: Lesson, module, chapter, course

**Activity**:
A standalone, linkable classroom exercise focused on one learning objective and one interaction mechanic.
_Avoid_: Game, play, widget, test, exam

**Attendee**:
A workshop participant opening an Activity link on a phone or laptop during or after a Session.
_Avoid_: User, player, student, customer

**Trainer**:
The facilitator leading the live Session and sharing Activity links at specific points in the agenda.
_Avoid_: Admin, teacher, instructor, host

### Exercise mechanics

**Mechanic**:
The rule set governing how an Attendee responds inside an Activity: Choice, MythFact, Triage, Matching, or DraftReview.
_Avoid_: Activity type, template, mode, plugin

**Prompt**:
A single workplace scenario, question, claim, or AI-generated draft presented to an Attendee for judgment within an Activity.
_Avoid_: Item, card, slide, problem

**AttendeeResponse**:
The structured judgment submitted by an Attendee for a Prompt, such as a chosen bucket, a pair selection, or a set of flagged draft segments.
_Avoid_: Answer, input, click, guess

**Verdict**:
The immediate pedagogical feedback returned after an AttendeeResponse, stating whether the judgment holds and why according to session principles.
_Avoid_: Toast, alert, answer key, score popup
