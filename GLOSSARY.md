# AI at Work Classroom Activities

Interactive workshop activities accompanying the Arabic "Introduction to AI in Daily Work" training series for university students and professionals.

## Language

### Workshop structure

**Session**:
A scheduled workshop unit in the five-part training series, grouping the section checkpoints used during that class.
_Avoid_: Lesson, module, chapter, course

**Activity**:
A standalone, linkable post-section checkpoint accompanying one main part of a Session, combining a mixture of interactive mechanics to reinforce the skills taught in that part and concluding with an Arabic celebration screen.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, quiz, worksheet

**Attendee**:
A workshop participant opening an Activity link on a phone or laptop during or after a Session.
_Avoid_: User, player, student, customer

**Trainer**:
The facilitator leading the live Session and sharing Activity links after each presentation part.
_Avoid_: Admin, teacher, instructor, host

### Exercise mechanics

**Mechanic**:
The rule set governing how an Attendee interacts with a Prompt: BucketSort, Matching, Sequence, DraftReview, MythFact, OddOneOut, or Choice.
_Avoid_: Activity type, template, mode, plugin

**Prompt**:
A single category-sorting deck, pair-matching set, step-ordering flow, AI-generated draft memo, myth-or-fact claim, odd-one-out group, or workplace roleplay scenario presented to an Attendee within an Activity.
_Avoid_: Question row, exam item, slide

**AttendeeResponse**:
The structured action submitted by an Attendee for a Prompt, such as sorting an item into a category bucket, connecting a concept-example pair, ordering workflow steps, highlighting a draft segment, stamping myth or fact, spotting the odd item out, or choosing a workplace action.
_Avoid_: Answer, input, guess

**Verdict**:
The immediate pedagogical feedback returned after an AttendeeResponse, stating whether the action holds and why according to session principles.
_Avoid_: Toast, alert, answer key, score popup
